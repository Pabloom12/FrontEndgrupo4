Mate National Festival

By Agustin Abreu

Welcome to a celebration of Uruguay's most iconic symbol, the Mate festival. If you want to know more about Uruguayan culture, this festivity is a must, because our country is known for having many traditions, but just a few are as representative as mate. But before we start, we need to know what a “mate” is.

The Mate is an herbal infusion that has its origins in Guaraní culture, since its beginning was something that created opportunities for the people to meet,talk and share moments together.

Now that we all know what a Mate is, we can see more about this holiday, starting with its origins: the Mate national festival started being celebrated as a local festivity at the San José de Mayo city, but with the years gained more visibility until it became a national and well known traditional festivity, that combines history, music, gastronomy and rural traditions, that’s why this celebration is one of the most significant ones in Uruguay.

The festivity usually takes place in late November, so the best you can do is inform yourself by visiting the San José government website, closer to October because it is not always celebrated on the same date.

There you can witness a lot of musical shows either national or international, you also have the “aparcerías” competition where different gaucho groups build traditional structures (like grocery stores, chapels or ranches) and compete to have the best period setting. There also are skill and field tests that include activities like riding, roping, rein tests and other traditional horse games. Another awesome event on the schedule are the parades, where the participants parade on horseback wearing gaucho attire, and even choose the “Flor de pago” winner (main beauty tradition that pays homage to the rural woman) as a representative of the tradition.

You also can taste some of the typical “Criolla” gastronomy, that is prepared on a stove.

In conclusion, the National Mate festival is much more than a celebration of a national drink, it is an event that preserves and shows to everyone the history, traditions and values of Uruguay. From music, gastronomy, rural competitions and cultural activities, the festival also connects the modern Uruguayans with their roots. If you are now interested in understanding Uruguayan culture, visiting this festival will be the best choice to make.
